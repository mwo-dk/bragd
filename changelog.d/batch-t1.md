| **Clubs keep their members, roles, IDs and ratings in one file you can share.** *Clubs* in the sidebar makes a Club or opens a Club file. Each member has a role (director, arbiter or member) and FIDE, national and Lichess IDs. The Club rates with Glicko-2 (the default) or Elo, and every rating a director sets by hand goes into an audit log with who, when, the old and new values and why. A lock stops two directors changing the same file at once. The FIDE, DSU and ECF rating lists are new Sources, off until you turn them on, fetched monthly by the Helper, and a new member can be found in them by name or ID. | [Clubs](clubs/clubs.md) | <!-- feature --> <!-- tour: clubs -->
