| **A Game's comments can be translated into your language.** *Settings → Language model* finds Ollama, Lemonade or LM Studio running on this machine and offers its models, or takes any OpenAI-compatible address. Then **Translate** under a comment puts it into your language, labelled as machine-translated, with the original one click away. A comment already in your language has no button, nothing is translated in bulk, and each translation is kept. A cloud address asks your consent once, and its key stays in the system's keychain. | [Language model](knowledge/language-model.md) | <!-- feature --> <!-- tour: model -->
