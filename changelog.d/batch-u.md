| **Bragd speaks only of the games you play, one notice at a time.** Notices and hints come one after another instead of all at once over the board. Slices, the formats on the Files tab, the Source priority, Settings areas and What's new show only what concerns your Game families. Sources are named as you know them (*The Week in Chess*, not `twic`), picked from a list, and an offline Bragd says so in words. The wizard folds the Sources it cannot fetch yet into one *Coming later* line. Disk watch has one name and one place, *Settings → Knowledge*; the file picker names Coxswain only when it is installed; counts read *1 Game family*. | [Help and notices](getting-started/help.md#why-do-i-see-only-one-notice-at-a-time) |
