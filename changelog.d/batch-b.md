| **The Helper starts with your session when you ask, obeys `BRAGD_OFFLINE`, and the install scripts install it.** Turning *Start with my session* on registers the Helper with your session, as `bragd-helper --service on` does, and turning it off takes it away. With `BRAGD_OFFLINE` set, no Source is fetched. `install.sh` and `install.ps1` put `bragd-helper` beside `bragd-gui`, and `install.ps1` keeps `%VAR%` entries in your `PATH`. | [Docs](knowledge/helper.md#how-long-it-runs) |
