| **Pair your own machines as Peers.** *Settings → Peers* shows a 6-digit code on one machine; type it on the other, found on the home network or by a name such as a Tailscale address. From then on their Helpers talk only to each other's key, over TLS, and show each other's name, version and whether they are reachable. A code lasts two minutes and five tries, and works once. Either side can unpair. Peers are off until you turn them on. | [Pairing your machines](peers/peers.md) | <!-- feature --> <!-- tour: peers -->
