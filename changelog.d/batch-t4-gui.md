| **A Club runs its own Swiss and round-robin Tournaments.** On the Clubs page, register and check in players, pair each Round by FIDE's Dutch rules or the Berger tables, show the pairings on a second screen, enter and correct results, take in late arrivals and withdrawals, and publish standings and a crosstable as one HTML page. A director can change any pairing: Bragd says which rules the change breaks and logs it with the reason. Standings use FIDE's tiebreaks (C.07, 2026) in the order you choose, starting from the Danish Chess Union's. Exports: FIDE TRF, PGN and a `.bragd-tournament` bundle. | [Tournaments](clubs/tournaments.md) | <!-- feature --> <!-- tour: tournaments -->
