| **A tournament night takes fewer clicks and less scrolling.** An open Tournament or League takes the Clubs page, with a way back. Enter adds a guest and returns to Name, and a pasted list adds a whole field at once. A correction or *Pair again* asks its reason in the page. A knockout ranks players by the Round they went out in, a Dutch hand pairing names the players who meet again, *Change the pairing* has White and Black headings, and tiebreaks read ½ and ¼ like the points. On a narrow window the Clubs tables show a block a row instead of running off the edge.
