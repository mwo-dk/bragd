| **Backgammon numbers the points as its moves do, and the opening throw says whose die is whose.** The board's point numbers follow the Side to move, so Black's `13/8` starts where the board says 13. On the opening throw a small checker under each die names its Side, and the turn line says *White starts: 4 against 2*. *Resign…* folds the three resignations into one choice and shows only against an Engine. Ur and senet list a turn as its throw and its move, `3 2: 5-7`. The install wizard's Engines step names the Engines it found and counts the rest, a Strip rule's empty fields show slanted examples, and Commentary asks you to stop a running Engine instead of to analyse. | [Backgammon](play/backgammon.md#point-numbers) |
