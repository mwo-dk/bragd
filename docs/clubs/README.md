# Clubs

A Club is the people who play together: its members, their roles and IDs, and the rating the Club keeps for each. Bragd keeps a Club in one file, so copying the file shares the Club. Tournaments come later in 0.3.0 and will belong to a Club.

| Page | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [Clubs](clubs.md) | Members, roles and IDs in one Club file, club ratings and their audit log, the lock, finding players in rating lists |

The rating lists Bragd can fetch, and those it cannot, are in [Rating lists](../reference/rating-lists.md).
