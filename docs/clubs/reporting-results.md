# Reporting results to your federation

When a rated Tournament or League is over, its results go to the rating body: FIDE, your national federation, or both. Bragd writes the files; you send them. Bragd uploads nothing to a federation. The sources, dates and quotes behind this page are in [Reporting results](../reference/reporting-results.md); every federation was checked on 2026-11-19.

## The files Bragd gives you

Open the Tournament and choose the **Publish and export** tab ([Tournaments](tournaments.md#publishing-and-exports)).

| You need | Press | You get |
|---|---|---|
| A FIDE report, or a federation that takes "the FIDE text format", a "Krause file" or a "FIDE export" | **FIDE report (TRF)** | a `.trf` file |
| The games, for a federation that asks for PGN | **Games (PGN)** | a `.pgn` file, one game per pairing, no moves |
| A crosstable to email or print | **Standings and crosstable (HTML)** | one page that opens in any browser |

Before you export, give each player their FIDE ID and federation, and set the Tournament's place, date and arbiter: the TRF carries them, and a rating officer needs them.

## FIDE-rated events

The federation registers the event with FIDE first, at least 3 days before it starts (30 days if players above 2700, or women above 2500, take part). Afterwards the chief arbiter sends the **FIDE report (TRF)** to the federation's FIDE rating officer, who uploads it to FIDE. It must be in in time for the monthly list; one not in by the third list after the event is not rated. The deadlines below are each federation's own, and are usually shorter.

## Europe

### Austria

Austria's Elo referee takes the result within two days of the end, uploaded with the Swiss-Manager file. Events not run in Swiss-Manager send the **FIDE report (TRF)** to the FIDE referee at the ÖSB (fiderating@chess.at in the 2011 rules). Register five weeks ahead.

### Belgium

The national Elo has been archived since July 2026; FIDE's rating is the ranking. Email the results within 3 days of the end; send the **FIDE report (TRF)** for FIDE. Register 14 days ahead, 21 if norms are possible.

### Czechia

The LOK takes only a Swiss-Manager data file, within 7 days. Bragd cannot write it. For FIDE, the federation reports through the FIDE rating server; ask your rating officer whether a **FIDE report (TRF)** will do.

### Denmark

Create the tournament in turnering.skak.dk 30 days ahead (40 if FIDE-rated). Within 7 days of the last round (3 if FIDE-rated), type the results into turnering.skak.dk; it takes the games as PGN, so save **Games (PGN)**. The system sends FIDE-rated events on to FIDE. By agreement, you can email the report to rating@skak.dk instead.

### England

ECF-rated events are usually run in the ECF's League Management System, which sends the rating file itself. Bragd cannot write the ECF rating file: its layout is not public. Run the event in Bragd and enter the results in LMS, or send them to your results officer with the **Standings and crosstable (HTML)**. Tell players their results go to the ECF.

### Estonia

The Estonian federation takes results electronically; its current rules could not be read. Ask the federation, and use the **FIDE report (TRF)** for FIDE.

### Finland

The chief arbiter sends the report and the full results to SShL on its competition report form. Apply for a SELO or Elo event at least a month ahead. Use the **FIDE report (TRF)** for FIDE.

### France

Ask for homologation from your FFE account page. The FFE's Papi file is not documented, so Bragd cannot write it. Send the **FIDE report (TRF)** to your FIDE rating officer for FIDE.

### Georgia

No reporting procedure was found on geochess.ge. Send the **FIDE report (TRF)** to the federation's FIDE rating officer.

### Germany

DWZ results are entered by your regional association's rating officer (Wertungsreferent); the DSB publishes no file for it. Send them the **FIDE report (TRF)** and **Games (PGN)** and ask which they take. FIDE-rated events go to elo@schachbund.com.

### Greece

The arbiter submits the event for evaluation through the arbiter login on chessfed.net. Use the **FIDE report (TRF)** for FIDE.

### Israel

No reporting procedure was found on chess.org.il. Send the **FIDE report (TRF)** to the federation's FIDE rating officer.

### Italy

Within 8 days of the end, send through the TORO web system or to fsi@federscacchi.it. The FSI takes the **FIDE report (TRF)** (Swiss-Manager calls it `FIDE_Export`) when every player has a FIDE rating. The arbiter sends the results; the organiser sends the forms and the fee.

### Latvia

No reporting procedure was found on sahafederacija.lv. Send the **FIDE report (TRF)** to the federation's FIDE rating officer.

### Lithuania

Register with the LŠF rating administrator on its form at least 5 days ahead (30 with norms), with the €5 fee. Send the **FIDE report (TRF)** after the event.

### Netherlands

Register the event in the KNSB calendar; the KNSB gives you an OLA login. Results must reach the Bondsbureau within three months, "in a form fit for processing". The KNSB names no file: ask whether it takes the **FIDE report (TRF)**.

### Norway

For the Norwegian Grand Prix, email the result to turneringsrapport@sjakk.no by the day after the event, as Turneringservice files "or other known formats": the **FIDE report (TRF)** is one.

### Poland

The national ranking is kept in PZSzach's Central Register; its upload format was not found. Use the **FIDE report (TRF)** for FIDE and ask your federation for the national route.

### Scotland

Chess Scotland's domestic route was not found. Members' games outside Scotland are emailed to grading@chessscotland.com.

### Spain

FIDE reports go by email to informes@feda.org through your regional federation, with the **FIDE report (TRF)** (FEDA calls it the Krause file), the IT3 form and the **Games (PGN)**. They must arrive at least 5 days before the list. Register 7 days ahead, 30 with norms. In Catalonia and the Basque Country the same FEDA rules apply to FIDE events; how the Catalan Elo takes results was not found.

### Sweden

Report the tournament in the "Anmäl ELO-turnering" form in member.schack.se. It is a web form; type the results from the **Standings and crosstable (HTML)**.

### Switzerland

For FIDE, send the **FIDE report (TRF)** to the SSB's rating officer within 7 days; register 30 days ahead on the SSB's form. The national list takes results in the Result Submission Center, typed in or as a CSV that only Swiss-Manager and Swiss-Chess write.

### Ukraine

Email the **FIDE report (TRF)** to ukrchessrating@gmail.com for FIDE. National ratings go to ukrchessrating@ukr.net.

### Wales

Tell your zone's rating officer, listed on the Welsh Chess Union's grading page, before the event, and send them the results.

## The Americas

### Argentina

FADA's site has a page for requesting and rating tournaments ("Pedido y computo de Torneos"). Its format is not stated. Use the **FIDE report (TRF)** for FIDE.

### Bolivia, Cuba, Ecuador, Honduras and Nicaragua

No federation site was found for these countries. Send the **FIDE report (TRF)** to your federation's FIDE rating officer.

### Canada

The CFC takes only its `.ctr` and `.tms` files, which SwissSys, Swiss-Manager and the CFC's Swiss Assistant write. Bragd cannot: the format is not published. Results in another format cost $5.00 a player instead of $3.00. For FIDE, tell the CFC office before the event and send the **FIDE report (TRF)**.

### Chile, El Salvador, Mexico, Peru and Venezuela

The federation's site did not answer. Send the **FIDE report (TRF)** to your federation's FIDE rating officer.

### Colombia

FECODAZ publishes a national list, but its rating page does not say how results are sent. Use the **FIDE report (TRF)** for FIDE and ask contacto@fecodaz.org.

### Costa Rica

The FCA's procedure is in its *Reglamento de Elo y trámites de torneos* on fcacostarica.com. Use the **FIDE report (TRF)** for FIDE.

### Dominican Republic, Guatemala, Panama and Puerto Rico

No federation site was found; these federations put their events on chess-results.com themselves. Send the **FIDE report (TRF)** to your federation's FIDE rating officer.

### Uruguay

Send the report to the FUA's rating officer with the **Games (PGN)**.

## Asia and Oceania

### Armenia, Singapore and Iran

No reporting procedure was found. Send the **FIDE report (TRF)** to your federation's FIDE rating officer.

### Australia

The ACF's submission rules are in its Ratings By-Law. For FIDE, send the **FIDE report (TRF)** to the ACF's FIDE ratings officer.

### Japan

Email japanchess.rating@gmail.com on the JCA's template: the event, the players with their JCA IDs and names in Latin letters, and every game. The **Standings and crosstable (HTML)** has all of it. Reports in by the 20th count that month.

### New Zealand

Send the results to the NZCF secretary and rating officer within three weeks, in the format the rating officer asks for. Use the **FIDE report (TRF)** for FIDE.

### South Korea and Taiwan

No federation site was found. Send the **FIDE report (TRF)** to your federation's FIDE rating officer.

## Questions

#### Why can Bragd not send the results for me?

No federation publishes an upload API, and FIDE's rating server is open only to rating officers. Bragd will upload to a federation only when it publishes an API and allows it.

#### My federation is not on this page.

This page covers the countries Bragd has a language for. Every other federation still forwards FIDE-rated events: send the **FIDE report (TRF)** to its FIDE rating officer.

#### My federation takes only its own file. Will Bragd write it?

When the federation publishes the format, yes. Formats asked about so far are listed in [Formats Bragd does not write](../reference/reporting-results.md#formats-bragd-does-not-write).

#### Do League results go the same way?

Yes, through the same federation. A League has no TRF export yet; send its standings, or each match's board results, as your federation asks.
